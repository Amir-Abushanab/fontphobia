About

Two bakers, one oven, and a starter older than the shop.

Little Levain started in a home kitchen in 2019 and moved to Mill Street three years later. We still mix every dough by hand.

Our flour

Our wheat and rye come from a farm 40 km away and are stone-milled the week we use them. Fresher flour ferments faster and tastes of something.
