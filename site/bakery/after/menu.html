Before you order

We work with wheat, rye, milk, eggs, nuts and seeds in the same kitchen. The notes above list ingredients, but we can’t guarantee any item is free from traces of allergens.

Bread freezes well. Ask us to slice your loaf, and toast it straight from frozen.
